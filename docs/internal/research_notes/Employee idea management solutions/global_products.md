# Global (English-market) employee idea-management, suggestion-system and continuous-improvement products: features, pricing, deployment, and fit for a mid-size Kazakhstan company (Modus Trade)

Research date: 2026-11-11. All "seen" prices were checked on vendor pages on 2026-11-11 unless stated otherwise. Most third-party price figures (Capterra, GetApp, Slashdot, pricing blogs) are unverified estimates and often conflict; they are flagged.

## 1. Which products lead the category (incl. 2024–2026 entrants), and how has the market consolidated?

### Takeaway
The category has two tiers. Enterprise "innovation management" suites (IdeaScale, Brightidea, Qmarkets, HYPE, which now includes Viima and Planbox) sell to large corporations by quote. Lighter self-serve tools (Ideanote, Viima/HYPE Boards Basic, Sideways 6 on Teams) and lean/CI tools (KaiNexus, Rever) are the alternatives. The market has consolidated heavily: HYPE bought Viima in 2021 and merged with Planbox in 2024, and planbox.com now redirects to HYPE.

### Cited Findings
- HYPE Innovation (Main Capital-backed) completed its acquisition of Viima Solutions (Espoo, Finland, founded 2013, "more than 10,000 organisations") in March 2021. — [Borenius](https://borenius.com/references/borenius-advised-hype-innovation-on-its-acquisition-of-viima-solutions)
- HYPE also joined forces with INNOSPOT in March 2021 (its third acquisition after HLP in Dec 2020 and Viima). — [HYPE news](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/news/hype-innovation-and-innospot-join-forces-with-support-of-main-capital)

- HYPE and Planbox "have announced a merger" (article last updated 25 Apr 2024). Planbox features are being folded into HYPE's AI tools, and HYPE group revenue is "nearing €30 million". — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/why-hype-innovation-is-buying-planbox/)
- On 2026-11-11, https://www.planbox.com/ returned a 301 redirect to https://www.hypeinnovation.com/, so Planbox no longer operates as a separate brand. — [planbox.com (redirect observed)](https://www.planbox.com/)

### Inferences
- For the pitch, "the market leaders are enterprise innovation suites priced by quote" is well supported. There is a gap at the low end for a simple, local-language "issue/idea → owner → status" loop.
- With HYPE now owning Viima, Planbox and Innospot, the "affordable" Viima tier is effectively a funnel into an enterprise vendor.

### Gaps
- No independent 2025–2026 analyst ranking was found (e.g., a Gartner Magic Quadrant for idea management). Roundups found were vendor-authored or affiliate-driven.
- Interact's acquisition of Sideways 6 was not confirmed by a press release.

## 2. Product-by-product: workflow, analytics, anonymity, integrations, deployment, languages, data residency

### Takeaway
All products cover submission, voting/commenting and staged review. They differ on always-on vs campaign focus, how well the author can see status, and integrations. Ideanote documents the clearest author-status loop (phases, custom statuses, auto-routing, notifications). KaiNexus and Rever are strongest on owner/due-date and impact tracking. None of them document integration with Bitrix24 or 1C. Russian UI is confirmed only for IdeaScale and Ideanote. Kazakh UI is confirmed for none.

### Inferences
- **Status visibility for authors** is best documented by Ideanote (phases, custom statuses, owner notifications, author tracking). IdeaScale and Brightidea have statuses but publish less detail. Rever and KaiNexus focus on ownership and due dates rather than author-facing timelines.
- **SLA/escalation** in the ticketing sense (response-time targets, auto-escalation when overdue) is not clearly advertised by any idea-management vendor reviewed. The closest are Rever's "owner and due date" and Ideanote's automations. This is a real gap that a custom MVP could fill, and it maps directly onto the owner's "silently forgotten" pain.
- **Campaign vs always-on:** the enterprise suites (Brightidea Program/Hackathon, IdeaScale campaigns, Qmarkets) are built around campaigns. Brightidea's Idea Box, Ideanote collections, Viima boards, KaiNexus and Rever support always-on capture. Modus Trade's case (problems plus ideas arriving all the time) is an always-on case.

- Kazakh-language support was not found for any product. IdeaScale's full 40-language list was not reviewed line by line for Kazakh.
- Brightidea's integrations and deployment details were not on the homepage; its integrations and security pages were not reviewed.
- Qmarkets on-prem availability and language list were not found.
- KaiNexus Teams integration is unverified on the vendor site. KaiNexus language support was not found.

## 4. Adjacent categories and how they differ

### Takeaway
Engagement/voice platforms (Culture Amp, Peakon, Viva Glint, Officevibe, Qualtrics) measure sentiment through periodic, often anonymous surveys. They do not track an individual issue through to resolution. Ticketing tools (Jira Service Management, ServiceNow) have SLAs and statuses but feel like an IT helpdesk. Feedback boards (Canny, Productboard) are built for customer product feedback. Microsoft Lists or Power Apps can be assembled into an idea tracker but need build effort. None of these alone gives the "employee submits → owner clarifies → author sees stage → management overview" loop out of the box.

### Cited Findings
- **Culture Amp**: no public pricing. Third-party estimates are about $5–9 per employee per month, with a minimum annual spend of around $4,500; other estimates are higher ($9–14). — [Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/culture-amp); [Compono 2026 guide](https://www.compono.com/articles/culture-amp-pricing-guide-2026)
- **Workday Peakon Employee Voice**: Workday bought Peakon for $700M in Jan 2021. Pricing is custom and reportedly starts around $20,000 per year. ### Inferences
- Engagement platforms answer "are people afraid to speak up?" in aggregate. They complement an issue tracker but do not replace one: anonymous survey comments cannot be clarified with the author or tracked to closure.
- Jira Service Management's free 3-agent tier plus a customer portal is the cheapest off-the-shelf way to get statuses and SLAs for internal requests. But its IT-helpdesk vocabulary, English-centric admin and lack of voting or idea features make it a poor cultural fit for "employee voice".
- Canny's public-board model (votes, statuses, "notify voters when status changes") is a good UX reference, but it is built for external customers.

### Gaps
- Officevibe/Workleap pricing page returned 404; no current price was obtained.
- Qualtrics EmployeeXM, ServiceNow and Productboard pricing and features were not researched in depth (no sourced data collected).
- Atlassian's official JSM pricing page could not be parsed. JSM SLA feature details are not confirmed from the primary source.

## 5. Can a Kazakhstan company buy and use these today? Sanctions, payments, data localization

### Takeaway
No source showed that Kazakhstan as a country is under the vendor exits that hit Russia and Belarus. Atlassian's wind-down, for example, named only Russia and Belarus, and US export controls target listed entities. So purchase is generally possible. The bigger blocker is legal: Kazakhstan's Personal Data Law (No. 94-V, Art. 12(2)) requires personal data, with no carve-out for employee data, to be stored in databases or digital objects located in Kazakhstan, and consent to cross-border transfer does not cure a localization breach. None of the global vendors reviewed advertises a Kazakhstan data centre. Only Ideanote (on-prem, single-tenant or data-residency "available") and HYPE (on-prem per third parties) offer a theoretical path.

### Cited Findings
- Atlassian paused new sales in Russia and Belarus in March 2022 and wound down business there completely from 31 Oct 2022. Kazakhstan is not mentioned. — [Atlassian blog](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/development/atlassian-stands-with-ukraine)
- US export controls act on specific Kazakh entities rather than the country. For example, BIS added the Kazakh firm Elem Group LLP to its Entity List. — [BES.media (RU)](https://bes.media/amp/kazahstanskoe-too-pod-sanktsiyami-ssha-v-chem-delo-i-zachem-kompanii-litsenziya-ot-knb-e1dea4/)
- In 2025 Kazakhstan's tax authority reclassified payments to a foreign cloud supplier as royalties rather than service fees after a desk audit. This has withholding-tax implications for foreign SaaS. — [Chambers: taxation of cloud services](https://chambers.com/articles/taxation-practice-of-cloud-services)
- Localization rule: Art. 12(2) of Law No. 94-V requires storage "в базе и (или) цифровом объекте, которые находятся на территории Республики Казахстан", with no exemption by data category, sector or volume. Clauses 13–14 of the implementing Rules (order No. 179/НҚ) require processing of restricted-access data via KZ-hosted digital objects and storage in a KZ server room or data centre. — [UPPERSETUP 2026 guide](https://uppersetup.com/en/blog/personal-data-and-localisation-in-kazakhstan-in-2026-law-no)
- Cross-border transfer (Art. 16) is allowed to adequate states or on four grounds including consent. No adequacy list is published, and consent "does not cure a localisation breach". Employee data is not carved out, and HR platforms are named among the most affected. — [UPPERSETUP](https://uppersetup.com/en/blog/personal-data-and-localisation-in-kazakhstan-in-2026-law-no)
- Recent amendments:
  - Law No. 155-VIII (13 Mar 2025) raised fines.
  - Law No. 231-VIII (18 Jan 2026) added Art. 19-1 on automated decisions.
  - Digital Code No. 255-VIII (12 Jul 2026) did not repeal Law 94-V.
  - Law No. 326-VIII (25 Aug 2026) rewrote Art. 12(2) and added registers and size classes.
  - Breaches must be reported to the authority within one working day. — [UPPERSETUP](https://uppersetup.com/en/blog/personal-data-and-localisation-in-kazakhstan-in-2026-law-no)
- Penalties under CoAO Art. 79 Part 3 (MCI = KZT 4,325): medium business KZT 1,946,250; large business KZT 2,595,000. Part 4 (with harm): large business up to KZT 8,650,000. — [UPPERSETUP](https://uppersetup.com/en/blog/personal-data-and-localisation-in-kazakhstan-in-2026-law-no)
- Practitioners note ambiguity about whether the law reaches non-Kazakhstan entities. A 2016 Ministry statement limited localization to processing "in the territory of Kazakhstan". — [Chambers: localization problems](https://chambers.com/articles/problem-issues-of-personal-data-localization-in-kazakhstan)
- AmCham Kazakhstan published a data-localization issue brief in May 2026, which indicates the business community sees the regime as burdensome. The PDF text could not be extracted for detail. — [AmCham KZ brief (PDF)](https://amcham.kz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026-05-15_AmCham_DataLocalization_Issue-Brief_ENG_v5.pdf)
- Vendor hosting statements found:
  - Ideanote: multi-region by default; data residency, single-tenant and on-prem "Available". — [Ideanote pricing](https://ideanote.io/pricing)
  - IdeaScale: unnamed co-located data centres. — [IdeaScale FAQ](https://help.ideascale.com/government-security-faq)
  - Viima: hosting included, location unstated. — [Viima pricing](https://www.viima.com/pricing)
  - HYPE: on-prem possible per GetApp and TrustRadius. — [GetApp UK](https://www.getapp.co.uk/software/91239/hype-innovation)

### Inferences
- A suggestion or issue system necessarily holds employee personal data (author name, department, sometimes sensitive complaints). A multi-tenant SaaS hosted in the EU or US would very likely breach Art. 12(2) for a KZ operator. This is a strong, legally grounded argument for a self-hosted or KZ-hosted solution, e.g. a custom module or an on-prem Bitrix24 box hosted in KZ.
- Buying is possible in principle (no country-level ban found). In practice, quote-only enterprise vendors bring USD/EUR invoicing, possible royalty withholding tax, and English-only contracts and support.

### Gaps
- No vendor was found that explicitly addresses Kazakhstan localization or offers a KZ region.
- The UPPERSETUP summary is a secondary consultancy source and was not verified against the official text on adilet.zan.kz. Have it checked by a lawyer before using it in a pitch.
- Microsoft's, Workday's and Culture Amp's commercial availability in Kazakhstan was not explicitly confirmed by a primary source.

## 6. Design patterns worth borrowing for a small custom MVP

### Takeaway
Several patterns map directly onto the owner's pains and are cheap to build:
- an always-on, mobile-first submission form with photo;
- automatic routing to an owner, with a due date;
- a fixed, visible stage pipeline with custom "why" statuses;
- automatic notifications to the author on every change;
- a kanban or board overview for management;
- simple impact tracking;
- optional anonymity;
- a clarifying-question thread between owner and author.

### Cited Findings
- **Fast mobile capture with photo** (Rever, Viima, KaiNexus): Rever's app captures a photo, then problem, proposed fix and expected result, and handles weak connections. Viima supports photo and video upload on mobile. KaiNexus accepts ideas by text message or email. — [Rever App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rever-score/id1114875070); [Viima product](https://www.viima.com/product); [KaiNexus mobile blog](https://blog.kainexus.com/customer/continuous-improvement-software/announcing-the-top-new-enhancements-to-the-kainexus-mobile-app)
- **Owner plus due date on every item; findings become tasks automatically** (Rever). — [Rever](https://reverscore.com/)
- **Phase pipeline plus custom statuses explaining outcomes** (Ideanote: Review → Rate → Act → Done; statuses explain why an item was archived). — [Ideanote phases](https://ideanote.io/feature/phases); [custom statuses](https://ideanote.io/feature/custom-statuses)
- **Rule-based routing by department or category** (Ideanote automatic progression; Viima categories notify relevant managers). — [Ideanote](https://ideanote.io/feature/automatic-progression); [Viima](https://www.viima.com/product)
- **Author notifications and digest emails on phase or status change** (Ideanote). — [Ideanote notifications](https://ideanote.io/help/notifications)
- **Kanban board as management overview, shown on meeting-room displays** (Viima kanban, KaiNexus improvement boards). — [KaiNexus boards](https://kainexus.com/continuous-improvement-software/features/improvement-boards); [Viima product](https://www.viima.com/product)
- **Impact tracking** (KaiNexus built-in impact plus ROI module; Rever's validated savings "by site, line and category"). — [KaiNexus impact](https://www.kainexus.com/continuous-improvement-software/why-kainexus/increase-the-impact-of-improvement); [Rever](https://reverscore.com/)
- **Anonymity configurable per campaign or idea** (IdeaScale). — [IdeaScale](https://ideascale.com/product-tour/idea-collection/)
- **Recurring-issue detection with AI** (Rever flags patterns across reports). — [Rever](https://reverscore.com/)
- **Meet users where they already work** (Sideways 6 and Ideanote capture inside Microsoft Teams). For Modus Trade the equivalent channel is Bitrix24, which local apps such as "Банк идей" already target, including auto-creating Bitrix tasks. — [Sideways 6](https://www.sideways6.com/integrations/microsoft-teams-idea-management-software); [Ideanote pricing](https://ideanote.io/pricing); [Банк идей](https://www.bitrix24.ru/apps/app/bario.app3/)
- **Gamification and recognition** (Qmarkets Q-optimize: leaderboards, badges, peer voting). — [Qmarkets](https://qmarkets.net/products/q-optimize/); [Qmarkets article](https://www.qmarkets.net/resources/article/best-continuous-improvement-software/)

### Inferences
- **SLA timers and escalation are the gap:** an MVP rule such as "no response in N days → escalate to the department head or owner" is rarely advertised by the global tools. It would directly address the "silently forgotten" pain.
- **Two item types in one pipeline** ("Problem" and "Idea") with the same stages (Submitted → Clarification → Under review → Decision → In implementation → Done/Rejected-with-reason) matches the owner's request better than innovation suites built for idea campaigns.

### Gaps
- No public UX screenshots were reviewed in detail. Pattern descriptions rely on vendor marketing text.

## 7. Fit for Modus Trade

### Takeaway
Global products are useful as a source of design patterns and as pitch benchmarks, but not as the deployed solution. They are priced and built for enterprise innovation programs, quote-only, English-first (no Kazakh, Russian only in IdeaScale and Ideanote), lack Bitrix24 and 1C integration, and are hosted outside Kazakhstan, which conflicts with Art. 12(2) of the Personal Data Law. A custom Bitrix24-adjacent module hosted in Kazakhstan is defensible on cost, language, integration and compliance grounds.

### Cited Findings
- Cheapest published global options:
  - Viima Basic: $79 or €89 per month for 10 users, +€7 per user, 2 boards, no Russian UI. — [Viima pricing](https://www.viima.com/pricing)
  - Ideanote: $899 per month for 100 seats, Russian UI, on-prem "available". — [Ideanote pricing](https://ideanote.io/pricing); [Ideanote languages](https://ideanote.io/faq/what-language-is-ideanote-available-in)
- Enterprise suites are quote-only; the one third-party data point for HYPE starts at about US$16,000 per year. — [GetApp UK](https://www.getapp.co.uk/software/91239/hype-innovation)
- No Bitrix24 or 1C integrations from global vendors were found. Local Bitrix24 Market apps exist but are basic, and one is rated 3/5. — [Bitrix24 Market: Есть идея](https://www.bitrix24.ru/apps/app/mcart.idea4cloud/); [Bitrix24 Market: Банк идей](https://www.bitrix24.ru/apps/app/bario.app3/)
- Employee personal data must be stored in KZ, and consent does not cure non-compliance; fines reach KZT 1.95–2.6M for medium and large businesses (Part 3). — [UPPERSETUP](https://uppersetup.com/en/blog/personal-data-and-localisation-in-kazakhstan-in-2026-law-no)

### Inferences
**Useful:**
- Ideanote's status and phase model and author notifications: the exact "author sees stage" requirement.
- Rever's "owner + due date + auto-task" discipline and fast mobile capture with photo. Useful for service engineers and installers in the field.
- Viima and KaiNexus kanban boards: the owner's single overview.
- KaiNexus and Rever impact tracking: a simple "effect/savings" field lets management show that speaking up pays off.
- IdeaScale's per-item anonymity: addresses the owner's fear that people will stop speaking up.
- Canny-style "notify everyone who voted when status changes".
- Jira Service Management's SLA concept (time-to-first-response, escalation).

**Not useful or not a fit:**
- Campaign- or challenge-centric innovation suites (Brightidea, Qmarkets, IdeaScale, HYPE): overkill for a roughly 100-person distributor, enterprise-priced and quote-only.
- Engagement survey platforms (Culture Amp, Peakon, Glint): aggregate sentiment, with no case-level follow-up.
- JSM or ServiceNow: the IT-ticket feel discourages idea sharing, and they are a separate system from Bitrix24.
- Lack of Kazakh UI, Bitrix24 tasks, 1C linkage and KZ hosting in all global products.

**Pitch line:** "Global leaders prove the pattern works (BASF, VW, Cisco and others use them). But they cost $10k+ per year, are English/EU-hosted, don't talk to Bitrix24 or 1C, and storing employee data abroad is non-compliant in Kazakhstan. We take their best patterns and add SLA escalation, which they lack."

### Gaps
- Modus Trade's headcount is unknown, so the cost comparisons above are illustrative (50 or 100 users).
- Bitrix24 on-prem ("коробка") hosting in KZ as the compliance path for a custom module was not researched here.
